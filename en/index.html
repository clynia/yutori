The luxury of deciding in your own time.

Yu­tori is a Ja­pa­nese word for breath­ing room: the lee­way that lets you act calmly. It is the op­po­site of the ur­gency under which most de­ci­sions about money are made.

With us, you get that breath­ing room back. We look at the whole pic­ture, think long term, and focus on pre­serv­ing what you have built. You de­cide on your own sched­ule, not some­one else's.

An unbroken circle: all of your wealth in one view.

Your wealth is in many hands. You will see all of it.

  • The bank offers you products.
  • The accountant files your taxes.
  • The lawyer handles whatever is urgent.
  • And you look at it when you find the time.

When an im­por­tant de­ci­sion comes up, you will make it with all the facts in front of you. That is what a fam­ily of­fice is for.

Tend to your work, and to what it has earned you.

Peo­ple who have built a prac­tice, a com­pany, or a ca­reer give it all of their time. The wealth it gen­er­ates is left wait­ing, and money left wait­ing rarely sits still: it gets scat­tered, costs you more than it should, or takes on risks no one has mea­sured.

If one of these rings a bell, we can start there.

  • An apartment that has sat empty for months because no one is handling the rental.
  • Cash sitting idle in an account, earning nothing.
  • A boat or a second home that is used a few days a year and paid for every month.
  • Loans between family members or with your own company, with no paperwork.
  • A company abroad, set up in twenty minutes, and no clear idea of its obligations in Spain.
  • An inheritance on the horizon, and nothing in order yet.

Time for your work and for your family.

While you run your business, your wealth gets a plan.

Busi­ness own­ers and pro­fes­sion­als. High in­comes, im­pos­si­ble sched­ules, and wealth that has grown with­out a plan. We bring the order you never had time for.

A succession that is planned and talked through.

Fam­i­lies. Founders near­ing re­tire­ment, sev­eral heirs, and de­ci­sions best made be­fore the cal­en­dar makes them for you.

Short careers, lasting decisions.

Ath­letes. Going from earn­ing lit­tle to earn­ing a lot in just a few years is the most del­i­cate stage. We help you or­ga­nize what you earn today with an eye on life after your ca­reer.

When success arrives, so do the offers.

Artists and cre­ators. We re­view every offer be­fore you sign. And you will have an easy an­swer for any­one who pushes: "I never sign any­thing with­out run­ning it by my team."

Inheriting, with someone by your side.

Heirs. Taxes, pa­per­work, and de­ci­sions you can­not af­ford to im­pro­vise, with sup­port from day one.

Everything in one place. The decision is yours.

We co­or­di­nate the tax, legal, and real es­tate side of your wealth, along with your bank­ing re­la­tion­ships, under a sin­gle plan for ev­ery­thing you own.

Organize.

  • What you own and where, in writ­ing. Fi­nan­cial as­sets, real es­tate, com­pa­nies, debt, taxes, ex­penses, fam­ily struc­ture, and goals.
  • What your idle as­sets cost you. Empty apart­ments, un­used cash, and as­sets that only gen­er­ate ex­penses. We ex­plain in writ­ing what each one costs you, and you de­cide what to do.
  • You call us, and we talk to ev­ery­one else. We co­or­di­nate your ac­coun­tant, your lawyers, and your bank, work­ing from of­fi­cial state­ments and deeds, not screen­shots. In­struc­tions to your bank al­ways come from you.

Protect.

  • When the Span­ish tax agency asks, you will have the pa­per­work. With the law firm San­tana Lorenzo Abo­ga­dos, we re­view your tax re­turns and plan your taxes within the law.
  • Your es­tate, pre­pared well in ad­vance. We plan your es­tate and fam­ily suc­ces­sion so that your wealth passes to your heirs in an or­derly way, with less room for con­flict.
  • Loans and con­tracts, in writ­ing. We for­mal­ize loans be­tween fam­ily mem­bers or with your com­pany, on mar­ket terms and with all the pa­per­work in order.

Decide in your own time.

  • In­vest­ments out­side Spain, re­ported. We sup­port you on the legal and tax side: struc­ture, con­tracts, fil­ings, and doc­u­men­ta­tion for each clos­ing.
  • Eight ques­tions be­fore you sign. We re­view the con­tracts, costs, tax treat­ment, and fine print of any offer. We do not as­sess whether in­vest­ing in a spe­cific fi­nan­cial prod­uct is right for you.
  • A snap­shot of your wealth, every year. Pe­ri­odic re­ports and an an­nual re­view that com­pares today's snap­shot with last year's.

What is urgent, what can go, and what can wait.

Financial assets, real estate, companies and shareholdings, debt and financing, recurring income and expenses, taxes, family and succession structure, goals, liquidity, and risks.

Be­fore tak­ing a sin­gle step, we look at ev­ery­thing. The re­sult is a writ­ten as­sess­ment.

Investing abroad, reporting in Spain.

If you de­cide to in­vest out­side Spain, you will know in ad­vance what to re­port and when. With San­tana Lorenzo Abo­ga­dos, we sup­port you on the legal and tax side: we help you weigh whether it makes more sense to in­vest as an in­di­vid­ual or through your com­pany, we put any loans in writ­ing on mar­ket terms, we keep the re­quired fil­ings on sched­ule, in­clud­ing Form 720 (Spain's in­for­ma­tion re­turn on as­sets held abroad) when it ap­plies, and we gather the doc­u­men­ta­tion for each clos­ing.

Everything in your name or your company's, and reported.

Offers will keep coming, but you won't review them alone.

A friend's startup, the once-in-a-life­time prop­erty, the mir­a­cle scheme mak­ing the rounds on In­sta­gram. Be­fore you sign, we re­view the con­tracts, costs, and tax treat­ment, and we tell you frankly what the fine print says. The de­ci­sion is al­ways yours.

The eight ques­tions we ask

  • Who is proposing it, and what do they gain?
  • Who regulates it?
  • What documents will you sign?
  • What does it cost, and who earns a commission?
  • How is it taxed in Spain?
  • When and how can you get out?
  • Are there conflicts of interest?
  • What will you have to report?

From the first conversation to the full picture.

Then we stay with you through whatever you decide to set in motion, with periodic reports and a review every year.

Before we begin, you will know what it costs.

Fees agreed in writ­ing. The as­sess­ment is a flat fee. If you de­cide to move for­ward, on­go­ing sup­port is billed as a fixed monthly re­tainer based on the scope and com­plex­ity of the work. The term and the con­di­tions for end­ing the en­gage­ment are set out in your pro­posal.

Your money never passes through our hands.

  • We do not hold or move your money. You sign directly with the bank, the fund manager, or the other party to each transaction.
  • We are not a bank or a product platform. We have no catalog to sell.
  • We do not provide investment services. If your situation requires them, you would engage an authorized firm directly.
  • We do not promise returns. Anyone who does is selling you something.
  • We do not engineer tax shortcuts. If a structure needs to be hidden, it is not for you.
  • We do not rush you. Important decisions are made with time to spare.

Questions before you begin.

What is a family office?

It is the of­fice that co­or­di­nates a fam­ily's wealth-re­lated af­fairs: real es­tate, com­pa­nies, taxes, suc­ces­sion, and re­la­tion­ships with banks and ad­vi­sors. The wealth­i­est fam­i­lies have their own. Yu­tori brings that same co­or­di­na­tion to peo­ple whose wealth does not war­rant a ded­i­cated of­fice, with­out pro­vid­ing in­vest­ment ser­vices.

Do you hold or manage my money?

No. Your money stays in your ac­counts, in your name or your com­pany's, and in­struc­tions al­ways come from you. We or­ga­nize the in­for­ma­tion, co­or­di­nate your ad­vi­sors, and sup­port you.

Will you sell me financial products?

We have no prod­ucts to sell. Yu­tori Cap­i­tal is not an in­vest­ment firm. If your sit­u­a­tion calls for in­vest­ment ad­vice, you would en­gage an au­tho­rized firm di­rectly, and the final de­ci­sion is al­ways yours.

Who handles the legal and tax work?

San­tana Lorenzo Abo­ga­dos, the law firm headed by Mar­garita San­tana Lorenzo. Yu­tori co­or­di­nates the work, and the firm car­ries it out.

How do you charge?

We agree on fees in writ­ing be­fore we begin. The as­sess­ment comes first, for a flat fee. Then, if you de­cide to move for­ward, on­go­ing sup­port is billed as a fixed monthly re­tainer based on the scope and com­plex­ity of the work. The term and the con­di­tions for end­ing the en­gage­ment are set out in the pro­posal.

What if I invest outside Spain?

We sup­port you on the legal and tax side: the right struc­ture, the con­tracts, the re­quired fil­ings, such as Form 720 (Spain's in­for­ma­tion re­turn on as­sets held abroad) when it ap­plies, and the doc­u­men­ta­tion for each clos­ing, so that ev­ery­thing is re­ported.

What do I need for the first conversation?

All you need to do is tell us about your sit­u­a­tion. Be­fore we meet, we will let you know whether that first con­ver­sa­tion has a fee. If you de­cide to move for­ward, we will tell you which doc­u­ments to gather and what the as­sess­ment costs.

You don't have to carry it all on your own.

Take your time and tell us about your situation. We will reply to you personally.
