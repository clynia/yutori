The luxury of deciding in your own time.

Yutori is a Japa­nese word for breathing room: the leeway that lets you act calmly. It is the oppo­site of the urgency under which most deci­sions about money are made.

With us, you get that breathing room back. We look at the whole pic­ture, think long term, and focus on pre­serving what you have built. You decide on your own schedule, not someone else's.

An unbroken circle: all of your wealth in one view.

Your wealth is in many hands. You will see all of it.

  • The bank offers you products.
  • The accountant files your taxes.
  • The lawyer handles whatever is urgent.
  • And you look at it when you find the time.

When an impor­tant deci­sion comes up, you will make it with all the facts in front of you. That is what a family office is for.

Tend to your work, and to what it has earned you.

People who have built a prac­tice, a com­pany, or a career give it all of their time. The wealth it gen­er­ates is left waiting, and money left waiting rarely sits still: it gets scat­tered, costs you more than it should, or takes on risks no one has measured.

If one of these rings a bell, we can start there.

  • An apartment that has sat empty for months because no one is handling the rental.
  • Cash in an account no one has checked for cost or tax treatment.
  • A boat or a second home that is used a few days a year and paid for every month.
  • Loans between family members or with your own company, with no paperwork.
  • A company abroad, set up in twenty minutes, and no clear idea of its obligations in Spain.
  • An inheritance on the horizon, and nothing in order yet.

Everything in one place. The decision is yours.

One plan for every­thing you own: the tax, legal, succession, and real estate side of your wealth, along with your banking rela­tion­ships, coor­di­nated from Madrid.

Organize.

  • What you own and where, in writing. Finan­cial assets, real estate, com­pa­nies, debt, taxes, expenses, family struc­ture, and goals.
  • What your unused assets cost you. Empty apart­ments, cash no one has reviewed, and assets that only gen­erate expenses. We explain in writing what each one costs you, and you decide what to do.
  • You call us, and we talk to everyone else. We coor­di­nate your accoun­tant, your lawyers, and your bank, working from offi­cial state­ments and deeds, not screen­shots. Instruc­tions to your bank always come from you.

Protect.

  • When the Spanish tax agency asks, you will have the paper­work. With the law firm Santana Lorenzo Abogados, we review your tax returns and plan your taxes within the law.
  • Your estate, pre­pared well in advance. Family succession and Spanish inher­i­tance tax, thought through early so that your wealth passes to your heirs in an orderly way, with less room for conflict.
  • Loans and con­tracts, in writing. We for­malize loans between family mem­bers or with your com­pany, on market terms and with all the paper­work in order.

Decide in your own time.

  • Invest­ments out­side Spain, reported. We sup­port you on the legal and tax side: struc­ture, con­tracts, fil­ings, and doc­u­men­ta­tion for each closing.
  • Eight ques­tions before you sign. We review the con­tracts, costs, tax treat­ment, and fine print of any offer. We do not assess whether investing in a spe­cific finan­cial product is right for you.
  • A snap­shot of your wealth, every year. Peri­odic reports and an annual review that com­pares today's snap­shot with last year's.

Investing abroad, reporting in Spain.

If you decide to invest out­side Spain, you will know in advance what to report and when. With Santana Lorenzo Abogados, we sup­port you on the legal and tax side: we help you weigh whether it makes more sense to invest as an indi­vidual or through your com­pany, we put any loans in writing on market terms, we keep the required fil­ings on schedule, including Form 720 (Modelo 720, Spain's infor­ma­tion return on assets held abroad) when it applies, and we gather the doc­u­men­ta­tion for each closing.

Everything in your name or your company's, and reported.

Offers will keep coming, but you won't review them alone.

The eight ques­tions we ask

  • Who is proposing it, and what do they gain?
  • Who regulates it?
  • What documents will you sign?
  • What does it cost, and who earns a commission?
  • How is it taxed in Spain?
  • When and how can you get out?
  • Are there conflicts of interest?
  • What will you have to report?

Questions before you begin.

What is a family office?

It is the office that coor­di­nates a family's wealth-related affairs: real estate, com­pa­nies, taxes, succession, and rela­tion­ships with banks and advi­sors. The wealth­iest fam­i­lies have their own. Yutori brings that same coor­di­na­tion to people whose wealth does not war­rant a ded­i­cated office, without pro­viding invest­ment services.

Do you hold or manage my money?

No. Your money stays in your accounts, in your name or your com­pany's, and instruc­tions always come from you. We orga­nize the infor­ma­tion, coor­di­nate your advi­sors, and sup­port you.

Will you sell me financial products?

We have no prod­ucts to sell. Yutori Capital is not an invest­ment firm. If your sit­u­a­tion calls for invest­ment advice, you would engage an autho­rized firm directly, and the final deci­sion is always yours.

Who handles the legal and tax work?

Santana Lorenzo Abogados, the law firm headed by Margarita Santana Lorenzo. Yutori coor­di­nates the work, and the firm car­ries it out.

How do you charge?

We agree on fees in writing before we begin. The assess­ment comes first, for a flat fee. Then, if you decide to move for­ward, ongoing sup­port is billed as a fixed monthly retainer based on the scope and com­plexity of the work. The term and the con­di­tions for ending the engage­ment are set out in the proposal.

What if I invest outside Spain?

We sup­port you on the legal and tax side: the right struc­ture, the con­tracts, the required fil­ings, such as Form 720 (Modelo 720, Spain's infor­ma­tion return on assets held abroad) when it applies, and the doc­u­men­ta­tion for each closing, so that every­thing is reported.

What do I need for the first conversation?

All you need to do is tell us about your sit­u­a­tion. Before we meet, we will let you know whether that first con­ver­sa­tion has a fee. If you decide to move for­ward, we will tell you which doc­u­ments to gather and what the assess­ment costs.

Take your time and tell us about your sit­u­a­tion. We will reply to you personally.
